Important compatibility note

VRFlex does not currently change Quest system-wide headset or controller tracking positions. The installed-app library and per-app settings profiles are implemented as companion-app features. They do not automatically modify Gorilla Tag, GILLA, or other games. Live trigger movement and the Y-button overlay still require a supported Quest-level or game-side integration.

VRFlex is an independent early-stage project within the AutoTouch repository. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Meta.
